# ZANABUN-01 — Prior research

NIST Technical Note 1297 (Taylor and Kuyatt, 1994) distinguishes measurement accuracy from repeatability and reproducibility. Lorenz et al. (2011) investigate social influence on collective estimates. Bikhchandani et al. (1992) analyze informational cascades. These studies precede our paper. The original contribution here is a source-specific evaluation protocol for Zanabûn, not a newly discovered statistical identity.
