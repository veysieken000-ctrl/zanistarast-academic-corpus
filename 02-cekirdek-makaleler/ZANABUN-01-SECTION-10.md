# ZANABUN-01 — Objections and conclusion

**Objection:** truth is more than a scalar measurement. Agreed: the model evaluates only a quantitative interpretation of witness coherence, not every form of knowledge. **Objection:** independent observers can corroborate without an external standard. They can increase rational confidence, but their evidential force depends on an account of error dependence and alternatives. **Objection:** different methods may be costly or noisier. The fixed-budget theorem holds intrinsic variances constant; outside those assumptions it can fail.

The core result is conditional but important: agreement alone does not establish external accuracy. A second knower contributes most when their method probes a failure mechanism not already shared by the first report. The distinction between repeatability, independence, and external validation makes Zanabûn's epistemic question researchable without converting a statistical model into a universal claim about being.
