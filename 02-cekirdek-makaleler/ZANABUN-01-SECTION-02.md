# ZANABUN-01 — Counterexample

Let theta denote a true quantity, W_i an observer report, K their mean, and V the mean squared deviation of reports from K. For any nonzero b, set every W_i = theta + b. Then V = 0, yet the absolute error of K is |b|. Four observers reporting 5 when theta = 0 agree perfectly but are all wrong. Therefore low dispersion is not a sufficient condition for external accuracy. This is a mathematical counterexample to a strong interpretation of witness coherence, not a refutation of the founder's entire epistemology.
