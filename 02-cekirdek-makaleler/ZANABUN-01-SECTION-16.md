# ZANABUN-01 — Disagreement and truth

The converse inference also fails. Let theta=1 and two reports be 0 and 2. Their mean equals theta exactly, so aggregate error is zero, while their population-form dispersion is 1. Therefore nonzero disagreement does not by itself establish falsehood or misinformation. The example does not show that averaging is always safe: outliers, adversarial reports, and incompatible targets require additional safeguards. It shows that internal dispersion and external accuracy are logically distinct and must be measured separately.
