# ZANABUN-01 — Revision record

The legacy ZANABUN-01 draft introduced the four-witness consensus counterexample and the basic shared-error variance model. A later legacy experiment introduced fixed-seed synthetic trials with different observer counts. The present revision adds a fixed-total-budget comparison of independent calibration groups, an identification argument for shared bias, a converse counterexample involving disagreement, explicit reference-standard uncertainty, a second-knower verification protocol, and a prospective empirical study design. All are parts of one article family.

Remaining work: wider literature review, independent code and mathematical audit, language and editorial polishing, a consolidated journal-format manuscript, and real-world validation if ethically and practically appropriate. No publication or submission is planned.
